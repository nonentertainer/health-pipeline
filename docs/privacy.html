health-pipeline: privacy policy & terms

Last updated: 16 November 2026

health-pipeline is a personal, open-source tool. Its OAuth client is used only by its author to sync his own health data. It is not offered as a service to other people.

Privacy policy

  • What is accessed: the signed-in user's own health and activity data (sleep, heart rate, HRV, steps, workouts and similar) through the Google Health API, read-only.
  • Where it goes: into the user's own private storage (a private GitHub repository and the user's own tracker). Nothing is sent to the author or to any third party.
  • No sharing, selling or advertising: data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.
  • Tokens: OAuth tokens are stored only in the user's own encrypted secrets and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Deletion: revoking access stops all syncing; the user can delete the synced data by deleting their own repository.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms of service

The software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, under the license in the GitHub repository. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
